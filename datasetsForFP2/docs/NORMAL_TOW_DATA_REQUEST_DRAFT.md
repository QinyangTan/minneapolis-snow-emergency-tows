# Draft — public data request for historical towing records

Status: DRAFT ONLY. Not submitted or sent.

Official request portal entry: https://www.minneapolismn.gov/government/government-data/request-public-data/

Subject: Existing electronic records of Minneapolis public-street towing, 2022–2025

We are preparing an academic visualization comparing the geographic distribution of recorded towing during ordinary winter conditions and officially declared Snow Emergencies. We request existing public electronic records for January 1, 2022 through December 31, 2025, preferably CSV or another machine-readable format, for completed tows originating on public streets within Minneapolis.

If available in existing records, please include:

- An anonymized unique tow-record identifier, and identifiers needed to distinguish an actual completed tow from an order, cancelled tow, ticket or duplicate record.
- Tow date and time, with timezone and an explanation of whether each date represents dispatch, pickup, impound intake or another event.
- Origin neighborhood, street block, coordinates or another publicly releasable origin geography. Please distinguish tow origin from the impound lot location.
- Original tow reason code and description, including snow-emergency parking enforcement, ordinary parking enforcement, street sweeping, collisions, disabled/abandoned vehicles, stolen/recovered vehicles and other reasons where those categories exist.
- Ordering agency, completion/status field and public-street versus private-property classification, if recorded.
- Snow Emergency name/identifier or operational day, where recorded.
- The reason/status data dictionary, known coverage gaps, changes in collection systems or definitions, geocoding limitations, and whether private towing contractors or owner-requested accident recovery are outside the system.

We do not request owner/driver names, contact information, license plates, VINs, payment details or other personal identifiers.

If record-level data are not releasable, please advise whether existing aggregates by neighborhood, calendar date and tow reason/status are available. Please identify suppressed or missing values rather than coding them as zero. We are requesting existing records, not asking the City to create a new analysis.

Separately, please identify the complete official Snow Emergency declaration and operation calendar for 2022–2025, including start/end timestamps and any exceptions or extended winter parking restrictions. The publicly downloaded event layers do not necessarily cover all declared events.

Please advise of any fees before processing a chargeable request, and identify the appropriate record custodian if another department maintains these records.
