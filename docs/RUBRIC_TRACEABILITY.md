## Immediate FP1 gaps

- Existing 13-page FP1 proposal is useful research evidence but NOT the required **7–10 minute slideshow**. Build the PDF slides, rehearse and submit through the Canvas group assignment.
- Explicitly show data model: source, examples, nominal event/neighborhood, ordinal day, temporal time, quantitative counts, location geometry, missingness and 68 unmatched map points.
- Show three questions with **three analyses each**. Three visual findings must involve at least three attributes; do not substitute a citywide Day 1–3 bar chart as a stand-alone multivariate analysis.
- Present **drawn alternative visualization concepts** (e.g. raw dots vs neighborhood choropleth, animated day map vs common-scale small multiples, count vs 100% stacked composition) and explain the selected choices.
- Ask concrete feedback questions. Five real members should be ready to explain any code/analytic finding.
