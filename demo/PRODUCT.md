# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Minneapolis residents and new UMN students.

## Product Purpose
Explore how recorded tows change across Snow Emergency days and neighborhoods.

## Positioning
A data story with a freely rotatable city, exact HTML values and consistent comparison scales.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 10 selected three-day events, 2022–2025; 6,118 records, 87 neighborhoods.
- 6,050 spatial matches. 68 unmatched records remain in city totals.
- Count height: 0–66 across all events. Share: day count divided by the same neighborhood’s three-day total, 0–100%. A zero total has an undefined share.
- Event, day and neighborhood selection; three-day unfolding; two-event comparison; top-five highlights; neighborhood history; NOAA MSP weather context; CSV downloads.
- Drag, scroll/pinch and keyboard camera navigation. Reset view preserves data choices.
- Narrow screens use one city; exact comparison values remain in HTML. WebGL fallback provides SVG geography and HTML records.
- Counts are historical recorded volume, not individual risk. Station weather is not neighborhood snowfall or causal evidence. This is a selected sample, not a census.

## Brand Commitments
One Minneapolis winter theme: snow white, ice blue, navy. The map carries the detail. Keep supporting copy short and retain units, denominators and limitations.

## Evidence on Hand
`public/data/snow-emergencies.json`, `public/data/snow-emergencies-provenance.json`, `src/`, `tests/`, and `../docs/screenshots/winter/`.

## Product Principles
Use real geometry. Use a shared zero-based scale. Preserve exact values and source limitations. Keep secondary controls behind simple disclosures.
